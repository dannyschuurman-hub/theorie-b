Theorie B support

Theorie B helps you practise for the Dutch car theory test, driving licence category B. The app is available in Dutch and English. A mock test has 50 questions and a 30-minute time limit.

What is free?

You get one round of 10 practice questions and your first mock test for free, including explanations, a review of your mistakes and a scorecard.

What do I get with the purchase?

You get access to all 200 original practice questions with explanations and references to the law, plus unlimited practice and mock tests. You pay once through Apple. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Tap 'Restore purchase' on the purchase page. Use the same Apple Account you used for the purchase. You need an internet connection.

How do I request a refund?

Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I report an error in a question?

Tap 'Report an error' next to the question or email dannyschuurman@gmail.com. With 'Report an error', you can review your report before sending the email yourself.

Are these CBR questions?

No. Theorie B uses its own questions based on the law and has no connection with the CBR. The app does not guarantee that you will pass. The law takes precedence.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes, practice questions and mock tests work offline. You need an internet connection to buy or restore a purchase.

Which iPhone do I need?

You need an iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

How do I turn the exam reminder on or off?

Tap 'Remind me' and allow notifications. You receive one local notification, 3 days before your exam. You can turn it off in your iPhone's Settings or clear your exam date in the app.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone to remove all local data. Your purchase remains linked to your Apple Account.

Contact

Have another question? Email Danny Schuurman, the maker and seller of Theorie B, at dannyschuurman@gmail.com. You will usually receive a reply within a few working days.
