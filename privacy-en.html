Theorie B privacy policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Theorie B is an iPhone app for practising for the Dutch car theory test, driving licence category B. Danny Schuurman makes and sells the app as a private individual in the Netherlands. You can contact him at dannyschuurman@gmail.com.

Your data stays on your iPhone

You do not need an account or login. The app has no adverts, analytics, tracking or server of its own, and sends nothing itself.

Your progress, answers, results, language choice, exam date, reminder setting and purchase status are stored only on your iPhone. Danny cannot access them.

Your exam reminder

The app only asks for notification permission when you tap 'Remind me'. You receive one local notification, 3 days before your exam. You can turn the reminder off in your iPhone's Settings or clear your exam date in the app.

Purchases through Apple

The app offers a one-off in-app purchase through Apple. Apple processes the payment. Danny receives no payment details, name or email address. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that processing.

You can restore your purchase using 'Restore purchase' on the purchase page with the same Apple Account.

Reporting an error

You can tap 'Report an error' for any question. This opens Apple's Mail screen with the question number, topic, legal provision, your selection of what is wrong, your explanation, app version and language. You can see everything before sending the email yourself.

If you send the email, Danny receives it with your email address as the sender. He uses it only to improve the question and reply if needed. He does not share it with anyone and deletes it once the report has been dealt with, within 12 months at the latest. You can request earlier deletion at dannyschuurman@gmail.com.

Sharing your scorecard

You share your scorecard through the iOS share sheet. You choose who to share it with. The app receives nothing back.

Deleting your data

Your local data stays on your iPhone until you delete the app. Delete the app to remove all local data. Your purchase remains linked to your Apple Account.

Your rights

You can email dannyschuurman@gmail.com to request access to, correction or deletion of personal data Danny holds about you, such as an error report you have sent. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Young people

The app does not ask anyone for personal data, including young people. Theorie B is suitable for anyone studying for the theory test.

Changes

If this privacy policy changes, the new effective date will appear at the top of this page.
